Hey, thanks for stopping by.

I work as a cloud engineer and architect. When I spot something broken, at work or at home, I find it hard to leave it alone until I have fixed it. Most posts here start that way, with a problem I ran into and kept digging at until I understood it.

Outside of work I spend a lot of time on home automation and on making my computer fit the way I work. Most of the tools below came out of that.

## Things I made

### macOS apps

- [MicGuard](https://github.com/pszypowicz/MicGuard) - a menu bar app that stops Bluetooth headphones from taking over my default microphone.
- [cyclist](https://github.com/pszypowicz/cyclist) - a text-only app switcher in the style of alt-tab.
- [BrightSync](https://github.com/pszypowicz/BrightSync) - copies the brightness of the built-in display to external displays over DDC/CI.
- [sequester](https://github.com/pszypowicz/sequester) - an SSH agent that keeps keys in the Secure Enclave and sets forwarding rules per key.
- [TrackpadKit](https://github.com/pszypowicz/TrackpadKit) - a trackpad gesture recognizer for AppKit, built on raw touch events. I use it in [my fork of Ghostty](https://github.com/pszypowicz/ghostty).

### Terminal tools

- [pager](https://github.com/pszypowicz/pager) - macOS notifications that focus the right window on click and clear themselves, sent from a shell or a tmux hook. I wrote about it in [this post](/p/An-on-call-pager-for-claude-code-sessions-in-tmux/).
- [afm-summarize](https://github.com/pszypowicz/afm-summarize) - summarizes stdin into one short sentence with Apple's on-device Foundation Models.
- [optiprime](https://github.com/pszypowicz/optiprime) - a terminal UI that keeps a directory of Azure DevOps repositories in sync.

### Home Assistant

- [ampio-homeassistant](https://github.com/pszypowicz/ampio-homeassistant) - an integration for the Ampio smart home system.
- [ampio-mqtt](https://github.com/pszypowicz/ampio-mqtt) - the async Python client that the integration uses to talk to Ampio over MQTT.
- [scene_state](https://github.com/pszypowicz/scene_state) - tracks whether a scene is active.
- [state-card-feature](https://github.com/pszypowicz/state-card-feature) - shows the entity state inside a card.

### This site

- [hugo-theme-pager](https://github.com/pszypowicz/hugo-theme-pager) - the monospace theme that this blog uses. It fits the first page load into 14 KB.
